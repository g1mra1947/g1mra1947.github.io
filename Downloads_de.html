Members-only downloads page

This members' area contains encrypted files. To access them, click on the link to download a copy to your device, and then open the file.

When you open the download you will be prompted for a password that you can find in your hard-copy of the most recent Newsletter & Journal issue, or request the password by emailing web@g1mra.com.

If you want to download a lot of files, you may find it easier to request non-encrypted versions of the files in blocks using this form. The files will be supplied to you using the WeTransfer service, which does not require you to have an account: you will simply receive an email containing a link from us.

Do not share these files or passwords
