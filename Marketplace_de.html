G1MRA member to member marketplace

This is a service for G1MRA members only.

We host anonymised classified advertisements of items for sale, and invite enquiries.

The way it works is this: if you have something to sell, then please fill in this form. We will then post your item here but without any personal details. If you want to enquire about an item or make an offer to purchase, please fill in this form. We will then pass on your details to the vendor.

The vendor can then make their own decision as to whether to contact you and complete a transaction.

G1MRA will only provide contact details and will not offer any form of mediation service or any guarantee.

Prices shown here do not include shipping.

As elsewhere on this site, click on an image to see the full resolution version.
