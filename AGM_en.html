Autumn Show and AGM

The 2026 autumn show and AGM take place on Saturday 7 November from 10.00 to 16.00 at the British Motor Museum, Gaydon, Warwickshire CV35 0BJ, just off Junction 12 of the M40 motorway.

Entry is £10 discounted to £8 for G1MRA members. Accompanied children (16 and under) have free entry.
