This superb model appears to part of a rake of North Western coaches made by Mr BM Jervis sometime before 1915, when he exhibited them and won a Prize at that year's Model Railway Exhibition.


This coach, No. 5105, appears to be a partner to a coach exhibited by Mr H B Jervis at the 1915 Model Railway Exhibition.




In this picture, the coach number appears to be '5104'. The article tells us that this model, along with several others, were 'prizewinning' exhibits. The wherabouts of No. 5104 is presently unknown.



Here is the coach in company with Abergavenny and a short train.
