Mr William 'Bill' Franklin is said to have been Bassett-Lowke's first apprentice. That would presumably have been around 1908 although no actual dates are known at present. Bill was, or became, a skilled model maker and some of his work has survived to be handed down to us. This superb Gauge 2 bogie wagon forms part of a complete Engineer's train and is an example of his work. 

 



Here's the complete train lined up behind Henry Greenly's 'Great Northern Type' 0-4-0 tank:




The wagons are very much to Bassett Lowke style, but with more detail and crispness of assembly:




The component parts are undoubtedly B-L, so we can conclude that the vehicles were actually built by Bill Franklin himself.




Altogether a superb set of vehicles and a tribute to their builder, so long ago.
