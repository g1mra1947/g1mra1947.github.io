The East Coast Model Railway

This magnificent photograph, from Dr. David Turner, "Promoting the Main line with Models", shows a Gauge 2 LNWR Experiment built for 3 rail operation by Bassett Lowke heading a train of wood construction coaches on the London to Scotland model railway in about 1908.



The picture depicts a North Western engine on the East Coast main line, a very unlikely occurrence, but does at least show us a model which has a modern counterpart, or even perhaps the same loco.

Bassett Lowke built a magnificent trio of East Coast 'Atlantic' models for this layout and they are illustrated in the 1911 catalogue, by which time they all had been destroyed in the ferocious Brussels Exhibition fire in 1910. Here they are:



Note the North Eastern Atlantic on the right: Although this model was lost, a very similar live steam version sits on my desk as I write this.
