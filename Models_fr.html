Greenly starter engine fully built and ready to run but unpainted - £250

This Greenly starter engine has full radio control with 'cruise control', a powerful 5-pole motor, a flickering firebox light and a smoke generator that puffs. The engine will run for a couple of hours on a single charge and can pull 30 axles at speed with ease.

As a short wheelbase 0-4-0 it can traverse 60cm radius curves: perfect for going round the Christmas tree. The model is designed for robustness and is ideal for youngsters, but in an industrial livery the engine does not look out of place on an exhibition Gauge One layout.

You can learn more about the design, and read a detailed build guide (which includes example liveries) on our designs page.

Please note that these ready-to-run engines are constructed by a team of volunteers at our occasional 'build days'. As a result stock levels are very variable, and when a batch is sold there may be a lead time of some weeks before we can supply you. As a result, please do place an order using this form, but do not make a payment until we request it.
