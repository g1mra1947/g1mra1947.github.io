G1MRA groups

G1MRA has many local groups which range in size from a few friends who run together, up to large scale groups which have their own separate subscription and committee structure, run exhibitions and manage portable tracks.

Groups arrange their own meetings, many of which are in private gardens. This is a public website, so we do not publish names or addresses for these events. The best way to find out about activities in your area is to locate your nearest group, and then email the group contact using the address in the listing below. Alternatively, email enquiries to the G1MRA groups coordinator as groups@g1mra.com.

We also have global special interest groups such as the Gauge One 3D Circle which are not limited to geographical regions. These kinds of groups operate mostly via email and electronic forums, and have members all over the world.

If you are a group coordinator and wish to update your entry, please email web@g1mra.com.
