Galeries

G1MRA organises a public Spring meeting and an Autumn AGM and exhibition each year. We attend other public shows by invitation, often with one of our portable tracks.

G1MRA groups also arrange numerous members-only meetings, many of which are in private gardens. This is a public website, so we do not publish names or addresses for these events here. To find out about activities in your area, locate your nearest group from this list and then email that group's contact quoting your membership number. You may also email the G1MRA committee officer for groups as groups@g1mra.com.

You will find information about recent past events on this page.

If you have an event that you would like to add to this list, please complete this form.
